Key Pipeline Insights

1. Late Aircraft Domino Effect Late aircraft accounts for 23.47 mins average delay, representing the largest single contributor to flight disruptions.
2. Seasonal Summer Surges June experiences the highest average departure delay (13.99 mins), whereas September and October demonstrate negative arrival delay baselines.
3. Monday Morning Commute Mondays register the highest weekly departure delay average (10.87 mins), while Saturdays record the lowest (7.83 mins).
